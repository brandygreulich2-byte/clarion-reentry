My first 72 hours home

This printable guide helps you make a practical plan. Your situation may differ. Follow your specific release and supervision instructions.

Before release

  • ☐ Confirm where I will sleep tonight and how I will get there
  • ☐ Review any reporting instructions and deadlines with the appropriate agency
  • ☐ Ask about prescriptions, medication continuity and upcoming appointments
  • ☐ Gather available identification and release paperwork
  • ☐ Save important phone numbers and arrange a working phone if possible

First 24 hours

  • ☐ Get to a safe place to stay
  • ☐ Follow my individual supervision/reporting instructions, if applicable
  • ☐ Obtain food, water, clothing and necessary medication
  • ☐ Contact a trusted support person or recovery support
  • ☐ If I have nowhere safe to go, contact PA 211 for local options

Days 2–3

  • ☐ Confirm treatment, health care or recovery appointments
  • ☐ Review benefits eligibility and apply if needed through COMPASS
  • ☐ Start identification replacement steps if documents are missing
  • ☐ Contact PA CareerLink about employment and training
  • ☐ Make a plan for transportation to upcoming appointments

Help and official starting points

  • Emergency: 911
  • Suicide or emotional crisis: call or text 988
  • Local service navigation: call 211 or visit PA 211
  • Public benefits: COMPASS
  • Employment: PA CareerLink
