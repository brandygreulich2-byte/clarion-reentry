Reentry Programs & Services

Explore common reentry support activities and how to seek them. These descriptions are informational; they do not represent a promise that any particular class, program, staff member or opening is available.

Recovery Support Services

Peer recovery support, goal setting, resource navigation and recovery planning. Contact AICDAC to ask about current enrollment and availability.

Moral Reconation Therapy (MRT)

Structured cognitive-behavioral group approach that explores decision-making, accountability and personal growth. Participation depends on the offering program; this site does not accept enrollment.

GED and Adult Education

Explore high school equivalency preparation, adult learning and testing options. Ask the appropriate provider about enrollment and eligibility.

College and Training

Discuss applications, transcripts, financial aid, career pathways and training opportunities with an education partner.

Job Readiness

Resume preparation, interview practice, job searching, workplace skills and workforce connections.

Discharge and Reentry Planning

Plan identification, safe housing, benefits, medical continuity, transport, support contacts and obligations before release.
