Verification workflow

  1. Identify the official provider website or other reliable published source.
  2. Contact the organization using a publicly listed channel, when possible.
  3. Record the contact date, public details confirmed, method and any uncertainty in the editor-only log.
  4. Get permission before implying partnership or displaying a logo.
  5. Update the public profile and its status; remove or flag outdated entries.
  6. Recheck periodically and whenever an organization reports a change.

Status language: Published-source checked / Directly confirmed by provider / Needs review / Removed. Do not publish staff personal phone numbers or confidential information.

This static GitHub Pages site does not accept submissions. A future public contact channel should be created and reviewed before posting it.
