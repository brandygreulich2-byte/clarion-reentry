For Families

Support someone returning home with a practical plan that respects their independence and your own boundaries.

Before they return

Discuss housing, transportation, expectations, communication and essential appointments. Avoid promising services that have not been confirmed.

First days home

Help locate food, medication, a working phone and transportation if possible. Ask what kind of help the person wants.

Ongoing support

Make room for recovery, education, employment and family connection. Agree on boundaries and a plan for stressful situations.

Printable Family Guide

For immediate danger call 911. For emotional distress or suicide crisis, call or text 988.
