About Our Mission

Immediate help: Emergency 911 · Call/text 988 · Community resources 211

Our mission

To make it easier for people returning from incarceration, their families and community partners to find understandable public information, prepare for release and connect with opportunities that support stability, dignity and long-term community reintegration.

What this project does

Brings together publicly available local and statewide resources, printable planning tools, educational information and opportunities for community collaboration. People can use the site before or after release, or alongside a qualified service provider.

Our principles

Accessibility, practical preparation, person-centered support, accuracy, privacy and second chances. Listings are informational, not endorsements. Eligibility and program availability must be confirmed with each organization.

Independence and privacy

This is an independent community resource project. It is not an official Clarion County government or jail website and does not claim sponsorship by listed organizations. It is not a clinical, emergency, case-management or secure referral service. Do not enter confidential client, health, legal or correctional records here.
